WHY THIS MODEL

What is exact, and what is only an estimate.

Exact: your task calculator uses the published assessment weightings you enter. NESA states that schools aggregate task marks using those published weightings to arrive at the school assessment mark submitted for moderation.

Exact: for HSC courses with an exam, the reported HSC mark is the average of the moderated school assessment mark and the HSC examination mark.

Exact: one-unit Extension courses use E1-E4 bands: E4 = 45-50, E3 = 35-44, E2 = 25-34 and E1 = 0-24. This page displays the equivalent normalised /100 scale.

Estimate: your current raw internal mark cannot by itself predict your moderated assessment mark because moderation depends on your school's HSC examination performance while preserving the submitted rank order. HSCninja school averages and E4 counts therefore provide context, not a deterministic conversion.
