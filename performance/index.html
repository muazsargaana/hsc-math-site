METHOD

What the estimate uses

School side: NESA uses school assessment rank order and relative differences, then moderates the group using HSC examination performance.

Raw marks: your task percentages are useful for tracking school assessments but do not directly convert to an HSC mark.

Rank model: your rank percentile is centred around recent school-course performance and adjusted above or below that centre. Historical top-band counts are shown as context, not treated as a conversion formula.

Band: Extension 1 and Extension 2 are one-unit Extension courses, so the official HSC performance bands are E1 to E4.
