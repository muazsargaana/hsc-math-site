WHAT THIS ACTUALLY MEANS

Rank drives the school-side estimate.

NESA: school assessment marks establish rank order and relative differences, then NESA moderates them using the school group's HSC exam performance while preserving rank order.

So: raw task percentages do not directly convert into an HSC mark. If you are around the middle of a 60-person cohort, your rank-based projection should sit around that school's historical middle/average performance, not around your raw internal percentage.

Important: an average is a mean, not literally the mark of rank 30/60. The model therefore treats the school average as the centre and adjusts above or below it from rank percentile.

Band: Mathematics Extension 1 and 2 are one-unit Extension courses, so NESA officially reports E1-E4. The page calls this simply your projected Band and shows the official E-code only as the result label.
